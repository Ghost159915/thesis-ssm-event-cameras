About 60 seconds. The baseline, S5-RVT, uses attention to look at space and an S5 state-space model for time. EventSSM, my first model, uses a ResNet-18 CNN for space and Mamba for time. PureSSM swaps the CNN for a bidirectional Mamba, so it is state-space all the way through; it differs from EventSSM in the spatial part only. SpikingSSM adds spiking neurons after PureSSM's temporal blocks; it differs from PureSSM in the temporal readout only. Everything is trained and tested on Prophesee Gen1, driving scenes with cars and pedestrians, scored with COCO mAP.
