About 60 seconds. First I reproduced S5-RVT exactly: 47.7 test mAP, matching the paper. Then I froze everything else, the data pipeline, the FPN neck, the YOLOX head, the loss and the evaluator, and swapped only the backbone. That makes it a controlled experiment: any change in accuracy comes from the swap and nothing else. One honest difference versus the baseline: my models trained at batch 4 instead of 8.
