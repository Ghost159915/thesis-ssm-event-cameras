Three spiking readouts split the cost of spiking into parts

A leaky integrate-and-fire neuron reads out each Mamba temporal block. Three versions share the same neuron:

cost of the neuron dynamics

Predicted before training: analog ≥ graded > binary

5 / 10

About 60 seconds. The spiking model adds a leaky integrate-and-fire neuron after each Mamba temporal block; the spatial backbone stays non-spiking, a hybrid like HsVT. The key design choice: three readouts share the same neuron. Analog passes the membrane value through, graded only fires when over threshold but carries a value, binary fires plain spikes. Going PureSSM to analog to graded to binary separates three costs: the neuron dynamics, sparsity and binarisation. I committed to the predicted order, analog at least as good as graded, graded better than binary, before running anything. Published spiking detectors report at most one spiking-vs-non-spiking number; none breaks it down.
