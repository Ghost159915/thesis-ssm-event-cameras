A published claim that did not reproduce

The paper credits SSM robustness to rescaling the time step at inference. With its own checkpoint and code:

  • it recovered accuracy at no tested point
  • at a true 10× rate it cost 9–13 mAP
  • the 200 Hz headline (39.8) was not reproducible; best 29.7

7 / 10

About 60 seconds. Event cameras produce more events when things move faster, so a detector must cope with rate changes. At ten times the true event rate, PureSSM keeps 69.7 % of its accuracy, the most of any model; EventSSM 63 %, S5-RVT 62 %. The original paper credits this robustness to rescaling the time step at inference. I tested that with the authors' own checkpoint and code, under two protocols. It did not recover accuracy at any operating point, at a true tenfold rate it cost 9 to 13 mAP, and the headline 200 Hz result was not reproducible under the published artefacts. The robustness itself is real; it is just there without the trick. Keep the wording soft: not reproducible under the published checkpoint and code.
